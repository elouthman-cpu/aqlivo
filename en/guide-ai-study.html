Using AI for study

Use AI as a tutor, not a replacement for learning.

A simple framework

  • Ask for explanations at your current level.
  • Test yourself before reading the answer.
  • Request examples and practice questions.
  • Verify important facts with trusted materials.

Bottom line

Use AI as a tool inside a deliberate workflow. The best choice is the one that reliably improves your real task at a cost and risk level you understand.
