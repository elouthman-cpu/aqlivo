About Us

AQLIVO is an Arabic-first content platform focused on practical uses of artificial intelligence tools. Our goal is to help readers understand what a tool does, when it is useful, and which limitations they should know before using it.

What do we publish?

We publish guides, comparisons, practical workflows, and simple tools that run in the browser. We try to separate facts from opinions, and we make it clear when information may change as products are updated.

How do we choose topics?

We prioritize topics that help readers make a decision or complete a real task, and we avoid content that simply repeats news or marketing promises without adding useful value.

Editorial independence

If the site uses advertising or commercial links in the future, those relationships will not determine our editorial evaluations. Any important commercial disclosure will be shown clearly on the relevant page.
