How to Improve AI Image Prompts

Last reviewed: September 2026 · General educational content, not a substitute for specialized professional advice.

When an image result feels random, the reason is often that the prompt describes the “idea” but does not describe the “image.”

Describe the main subject

Who or what should be the center of the image? Mention the approximate age, product type, or primary element clearly and without ambiguity.

Define the environment

A room, street, beach, studio, or simple background. The environment shapes the lighting, colors, and secondary details.

Define the composition

Close-up, medium shot, wide shot, top-down view, or eye level. Composition can change the image more than many aesthetic adjectives.

Describe the lighting

Soft window light, morning sun, diffused studio lighting, or neon night lighting. Choose one coherent lighting description.

Add the important constraints

If the product color, number of people, or background shape must stay fixed, state that explicitly at the end of the prompt.
