How to verify AI output

Reduce errors and hallucinations.

A simple framework

  • Treat confident wording as style, not proof.
  • Check names, dates, numbers and quotes.
  • Prefer primary or authoritative sources for important claims.
  • For high-stakes decisions, use qualified professional guidance.

Bottom line

Use AI as a tool inside a deliberate workflow. The best choice is the one that reliably improves your real task at a cost and risk level you understand.
