How to Verify AI Output

Last reviewed: September 2026 · General educational content, not a substitute for specialized professional advice.

Language models can produce an answer that sounds convincing even when some details are wrong. That means your verification method should change depending on the type of task.

Separate opinion from fact

A creative suggestion does not require the same level of verification as a financial figure or medical or legal information.

Check measurable claims

Dates, prices, laws, figures, and policies change. Open the original source or a trustworthy recent source.

Ask for the assumptions

Ask the model which assumptions its answer is based on. This can help you spot details that may not apply to your situation.

Compare more than one source

For important topics, do not rely on a single answer. Compare sources and prefer official documentation when it is available.
