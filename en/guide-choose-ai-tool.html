How to choose the right AI tool

A practical framework for comparing AI tools.

A simple framework

  • Start with the outcome you need, not the brand name.
  • Compare output quality on the same task.
  • Check pricing, limits, privacy and language support.
  • Run a small real-world test before paying for a long subscription.

Bottom line

Use AI as a tool inside a deliberate workflow. The best choice is the one that reliably improves your real task at a cost and risk level you understand.
