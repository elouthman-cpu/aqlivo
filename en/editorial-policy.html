Editorial Policy

Our goal is to publish useful, understandable content based on practical use or sources that can be verified.

Accuracy

We distinguish between stable information and information that may change, such as pricing and features. Changing information requires periodic review.

Testing and sources

When we describe a workflow or result, we aim to rely on direct testing, official documentation, or trustworthy sources. We do not present unverified claims as facts.

Corrections

If we discover a material error, we correct it. Readers can send feedback through our Contact page.

Advertising and commercial relationships

Advertising does not determine the ranking of our evaluations or recommendations. Any important commercial relationship will be disclosed clearly.
